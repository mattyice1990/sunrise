Tucson Roofs

Why Tucson roofs leak: monsoon, heat and sun

Most of the repair calls we get are the climate's fault. Monsoon season runs roughly late June through September, and it's not just the rain that gets roofs. It's the wind out ahead of the storm lifting tile and shingle, the dust that packs into valleys and scuppers, and then an inch of rain landing all at once on a roof that's been baked dry for nine months.

The rest of the year is the slow damage. A roof surface here can run well over 140 degrees on a summer afternoon and then drop 50 degrees or more overnight, day after day. That constant expansion and contraction cracks sealant, dries out pipe boots, opens up flashing at walls and chimneys, and turns old underlayment brittle. By the time a stain shows on the ceiling, the underlayment has usually been letting water through for a while.

If a storm did the damage, we photograph everything before we touch it and put together the documentation your insurance adjuster will ask for. We can't promise what a claim will pay, but we make sure the paperwork tells the whole story.

  • Wind-lifted tile and shingle after monsoon storms
  • Debris-packed valleys and scuppers that back water up
  • Heat-cracked sealant, pipe boots and flashing
  • Underlayment gone brittle from years of UV
  • Hail hits on tile, shingle and skylights
  • Photo documentation for storm and insurance claims

Ask Us Directly

How much does it cost to repair a roof in Tucson?

Cracked tiles or a flashing reseal, you're usually looking at a few hundred dollars. Once water's gotten in and we've got to pull and replace underlayment, it costs more. Either way the inspection's free and you get an itemized price in writing, so nothing blindsides you.

Can you fix a roof leak fast?

Yep. We keep room in the schedule for same-week repairs, and if you've got water coming in right now we'll get out and tarp it 24/7 so the damage stops while we line up the real fix.

Do you repair flat roofs?

For sure. Flat, tile, metal, shingle, we work on all of it. Ponding, blisters, split seams, cracked tile, coatings that have given out, that's everyday stuff for us.

Does homeowners insurance cover roof leaks?

If a storm or hail did it, a lot of the time it is. We photograph and document everything while we're up there so you've got what you need to back up the claim.

Should I repair or replace my roof?

Comes down to how old the roof is and how much of it's going. We'll get up there, take a look, and give it to you straight. If a repair's the smart move, that's what we'll quote you, not a whole new roof you don't need yet.

How do I know if the stain on my ceiling is a roof leak?

Not every ceiling stain is the roof. In Tucson a lot of them turn out to be an AC condensate line in the attic, a swamp cooler line, or a plumbing vent. Part of our free inspection is ruling those out, so you don't pay for a roof repair you didn't need.

Can you repair a tile roof without replacing the whole thing?

Most of the time, yes. We swap the broken or slipped tile, replace the underlayment in that spot, and set the tile back. If the underlayment is shot across the whole roof, a spot repair just moves the leak, and we'll tell you that instead of selling you a fix that won't hold.

What should I do while I wait for a roof repair?

Move anything that can get wet, put a bucket under the drip, and if the ceiling is bulging with water, poke a small hole at the low point so it drains instead of bringing the drywall down all at once. Stay off the roof, especially wet tile. If water's coming in right now, call us and we'll get a tarp on it.

Do you offer a warranty on roof repairs?

Yes. Every repair we do comes with a workmanship warranty, and we go over exactly what it covers before we start, in writing.
