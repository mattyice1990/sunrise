Ask Us Anything

How do I know if I need a roof repair or a full replacement?

Comes down to the roof's age, how bad the damage is, and the shape the underlayment's in. We look first, show you photos of what we find, and recommend the most cost-effective fix, which is often a repair or a coating, not a full replacement.

Do you offer free roof inspections?

Yep. Every inspection's free and you get photos and a written summary, whether you hire us or not.

Do you install metal roofing in Tucson?

For sure, standing-seam metal is one of our specialties. It reflects the heat, shrugs off UV and monsoon rain, and'll go 40-plus years out here.

What roofing material is best for Arizona heat?

Metal and tile both hold up great against the heat and UV, and coating systems are the move on flat roofs. We'll match the right one to your building, budget and goals.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most houses take 2 to 4 days depending on size, material and weather. We give you a real timeline up front and keep you posted every day.

Can you help with storm or monsoon damage?

Yep, 24/7 emergency response, temporary protection to stop a live leak, and we'll help document the damage for your insurance claim.

Do you work with HOAs or commercial properties?

We do a ton of HOA, multi-family, retail and commercial work, with phased scheduling, detailed service logs, and one person you call for every property.

Do you offer financing?

Yep. We've got flexible financing on approved credit so a new roof fits the budget, just ask us during your estimate.

Are you licensed and insured?

Sunrise Roofers LLC is fully licensed (ROC #358079), bonded, and insured for both residential and commercial roofing across the Tucson area.
