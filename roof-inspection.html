Ask Us Directly

Is the roof inspection really free?

Yep, our standard inspection's free, no strings. You get the report with photos whether you ever hire us or not.

What do roof inspectors look for?

We check the field material, whether that's tile, shingle or metal, plus the flashing, the valleys, anything poking through the roof, the drainage, and we get in the attic looking for signs water's been getting in. Then we photograph all of it.

Can you inspect for storm or hail damage?

Yes, that's a big part of what we do. Storm, wind, hail, monsoon damage, we'll assess it and get you the photo documentation the insurance company wants.

Do you do pre-purchase roof inspections?

We do. If you're buying a house here, get the roof looked at so you know exactly what you're walking into before you close.

How long does an inspection take?

Usually 30 to 60 minutes for a house, depending on how big and how cut-up the roof is.

How often should you have your roof inspected?

Once a year is the honest answer in Tucson, and again after any hard monsoon blow. Our sun and our rain age a roof faster than most places, and the small stuff is cheap to fix before it works its way into the underlayment. Ours is free, so there’s no reason to put it off.
